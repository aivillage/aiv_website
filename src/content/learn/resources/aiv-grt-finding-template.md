Use this template to turn eval evidence into a short GRT-style finding memo.

Required fields:

- Claim
- Evidence source
- Samples
- Reproducibility path
- Limits
- Severity/importance framing
- Affected model/system claim
- Follow-up action

The memo should be useful to a reviewer who needs to reproduce the finding, understand its limits, and decide on the next action without over-reading the evidence.
