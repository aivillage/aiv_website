This module helps learners interpret evaluation results without overclaiming. The curated resources support reading scores, comparing runs, inspecting examples, and identifying where results are too narrow. Your artifact is a short interpretation note that states what the eval suggests, what it does not prove, and what follow-up tests or reviews are needed. Use the module responsibly by looking at failures and borderline cases, not only aggregate scores. A result can be useful and still incomplete. Good interpretation turns numbers into a decision conversation about readiness, limits, and evidence gaps. Keep the interpretation short, cautious, and connected to the next decision the team needs to make.
