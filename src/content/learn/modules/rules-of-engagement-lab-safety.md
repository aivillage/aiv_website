This module is the public safety anchor for the Offensive AI Security and Red Teaming track. It does not teach exploitation steps; it teaches the boundaries that must exist before any lab or assessment. The required AIV template covers scope, authorized target, allowed actions, disallowed actions, data classes, stop conditions, reporting path, and debrief expectation.

Your artifact is a completed rules-of-engagement note for a controlled exercise. Use the module responsibly by keeping all practice inside intentionally provided systems or approved environments. Do not test real third-party systems, production services, or other people's data. The purpose is safer learning and better defensive review, not unsupervised attack practice.
