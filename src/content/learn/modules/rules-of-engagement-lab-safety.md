This module is the public safety anchor for the Offensive AI Security and Red Teaming track. It does not teach exploitation steps; it teaches the boundaries that must exist before any lab or assessment. The curated resources support a rules-of-engagement note covering authorization, scope, lab fixtures, data limits, stop conditions, and expected reporting. Your artifact is a short lab safety and authorization note for a controlled exercise. Use the module responsibly by keeping all practice inside intentionally provided systems or approved environments. Do not test real third-party systems, production services, or other people's data. The purpose is safer learning and better defensive review, not unsupervised attack practice.
