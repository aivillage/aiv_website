This draft module gives the future track its vocabulary. Security asks whether the system and data are protected against misuse, compromise, and abuse. Safety asks whether the system's behavior can create harm or unacceptable risk. Governance asks who owns the decision, what evidence is required, how review happens, and how limits are communicated.

Your artifact is a security, safety, and governance map for one AI use case. Include the use case, stakeholders, security controls, safety concerns, governance owner, review evidence, decision boundary, and unresolved questions. Treat NIST as voluntary guidance and AI Safety Atlas as educational framing, not as a legal or compliance conclusion.
