This module gives new learners a practical vocabulary for AI without assuming a math or programming background. Use the curated introduction to separate models, data, training, inference, and applications, then write down where a model is making a prediction versus where a person or system is making a decision. The useful artifact is a short learning journal entry that names one AI system, its inputs, its outputs, and the places where uncertainty still matters. Use the module responsibly by avoiding claims that a model understands, verifies, or approves something on its own. The goal is a grounded starting point for later modules, including data handling, evaluation, and security checkpoints.
