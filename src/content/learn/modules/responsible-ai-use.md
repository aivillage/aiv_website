This draft module turns broad responsible-use principles into a short workflow checklist. Start with one use case and write what the AI system may do, what it must not do, what data it may handle, who reviews higher-impact outputs, and when work escalates to a human owner.

Your artifact is a responsible-use checklist or workflow policy sketch. Include allowed uses, restricted uses, data limits, human review triggers, affected stakeholders, escalation path, owner, review cadence, and open questions. Note that principles are voluntary guidance; existing laws, rights, consumer-protection rules, contracts, and organizational policies may still apply.
