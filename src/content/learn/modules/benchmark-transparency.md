This module focuses on benchmark transparency for AI evaluation and research communication. The curated resources help learners ask what a benchmark measures, what data it uses, how scoring works, and when comparisons are misleading. Your artifact is a benchmark transparency note for one benchmark or eval result, including scope, limitations, and relevance to a real decision. Use the module responsibly by avoiding leaderboard-style conclusions when the benchmark does not match the deployment context. Transparent reporting helps reviewers understand whether a score is useful evidence, weak evidence, or the wrong evidence for the question at hand. Keep the note attached to the result wherever it is shared so benchmark limits do not disappear.
