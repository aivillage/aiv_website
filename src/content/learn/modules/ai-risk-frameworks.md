This draft module uses NIST's AI RMF and GenAI Profile as the primary framework spine. The learner chooses one AI system or workflow, writes a short description of the use case, and maps the system to framework functions, risks, controls, review evidence, and unresolved questions.

Your artifact is a risk-review note or single-system RMF profile. Include system purpose, owner, affected users, risk categories, current controls, evidence source, review cadence, and limits. State clearly that the framework is voluntary guidance and that a short mapping is not certification, legal advice, or proof of safety.
