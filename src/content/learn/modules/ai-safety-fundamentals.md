This draft module introduces AI safety concepts only far enough to support governance review. The goal is to name the safety concern, affected stakeholders, evidence needed, review owner, and decision limit. It does not turn the governance track into a cohort path or advanced alignment program.

Your artifact is a safety-to-governance memo or risk taxonomy note. Include the safety concept, affected use case, possible harm or uncertainty, evidence needed, owner, review cadence, and follow-up question. BlueDot, MATS, and ARENA remain non-core off-ramps or draft metadata, not required Learn resources.
