This draft module is informational and U.S.-anchored. It helps learners notice copyright and licensing questions that can appear in AI workflows: training data, retrieval corpora, generated outputs, copied examples, third-party documentation, open licenses, attribution, and source records. It is not legal advice.

Your artifact is a licensing/source-use note. Include source type, source URL or origin, license or rights signal, intended use, attribution plan, retention plan, jurisdiction questions, owner, and unresolved legal or policy questions. Laws, agency guidance, license terms, and platform rules change; jurisdiction matters and human legal review may be required.
