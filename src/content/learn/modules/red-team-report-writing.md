This module is a low-risk public anchor for turning authorized AI security work into useful defensive communication. It focuses on reporting structure rather than exploit instruction. The curated resources support writing findings that include scope, evidence, impact, affected assumptions, and remediation ideas. Your artifact is a red-team report draft for a controlled or hypothetical exercise, with sensitive details minimized to what defenders need. Use the module responsibly by honoring the rules of engagement, avoiding public disclosure of unreviewed issues, and separating confirmed observations from speculation. A good report helps teams understand and reduce risk without encouraging unsafe replication outside the authorized setting.
