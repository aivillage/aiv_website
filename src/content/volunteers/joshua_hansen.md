Joshua is the founder of ATSK, where he advises operators and investors on technical diligence and systems architecture. He has spent two decades building and operating cloud, data, and observability systems, including years delivering Splunk Professional Services. He has worked with  manufacturers, retailers, energy companies, financial institutions, credit bureaus, healthcare organizations, universities, and government agencies across North America, Europe, and Asia.

He led architecture and operations for a multi-tenant SaaS platform running Kubernetes across AWS and GCP. He designed and built its IAM/SSO and access controls, and treats security as part of engineering and operations, not a handoff.

Observability taught him to model how organizations and systems actually work. The same discipline now runs through his work on agent systems using local and frontier models: tool boundaries, approval gates, prompt injection, and the telemetry required to tell whether any of it is actually behaving. At AI Village, he serves on the steering committee, runs the website, and works on the infrastructure and operations teams.
