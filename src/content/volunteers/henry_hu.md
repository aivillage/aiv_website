Henry Hu is a cybersecurity leader and currently serves as Chapter Chair of the OWASP Taiwan Chapter. He also represents Taiwan in the Cloud Security Alliance and The Honeynet Project, serves on the Board of the Taiwan Digital Security Alliance, and is Deputy Secretary-General of the Cybersecurity Alliance, helping connect Taiwan’s cybersecurity ecosystem with the international community.

With extensive experience in enterprise cybersecurity, Henry specializes in incident response, digital forensics, security governance, compliance, and large-scale security deployments. He holds numerous internationally recognized certifications, including CISSP, CISM, CEH, CHFI, CCSK, and ISO/IEC 27001 & 42001 Lead Auditor/Consultant.

A frequent instructor and international conference speaker, Henry is also the founder of Auriga Security, where he leads a team delivering modern cybersecurity solutions, including endpoint, cloud, and managed security services, to help organizations strengthen their cyber resilience.
