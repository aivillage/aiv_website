Our fireside chat with Bruce Schneier from DEF CON 34 is now [available on YouTube](https://youtu.be/7-sMBqPV3XU). It was the one fireside chat we recorded inside the Village, and we're excited to be able to share it. A huge thanks to Bruce for taking the time to join us and share his insights on AI and cybersecurity.

Thanks again to everyone who came out and participated in [AI Village at DEF CON 34](https://aivillage.org/events/defcon-34/). We had a fantastic weekend, with 34 poster presentations, 12 DEF CON stage sessions, six fireside chats, two competitions, and live demos throughout the Village. There was a lot to see and do, and we're working to make as much of the event available online as we can.

You can browse the DEF CON 34 poster presentations in our [poster archive](https://aivillage.org/posters/#defcon-34). All of the posters whose authors gave us permission to host are available there.

DEF CON handled video production for the AI Village sessions held on DEF CON stages and is publishing those recordings through its official channels. We'll share and highlight the AI Village talks as they become available. We're also working on writeups and resources from HalCTF and AI Village Plays Pokémon: DEF CON Edition.

Our current plan is to share something new from DEF CON 34 each week as more material becomes available, so keep an eye out for more.

Want to get involved with AI Village? Visit our [Community page](https://aivillage.org/community/) to join the community, contribute, and stay connected.

We also designed HalCTF and AI Village Plays Pokémon to travel beyond DEF CON. If you're organizing a conference or event and would like to bring one of them, or AI Village more broadly, to your event, contact us at [team@aivillage.org](mailto:team@aivillage.org).
