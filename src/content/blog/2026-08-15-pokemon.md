2026 is the year of agents, with many people actively experimenting with building agentic workflows to integrate into both professional and personal projects.  However, many of the public guides and example projects have been lackluster.  People are taught to only use cloud models, and to just throw data and information at the model until it solves it.  Many tooling guides focus on how to do things like MCP, but not the why of which things in your workflow should be turned into a tool call, or how that tool call looks like in a local environement.  We built AI Village Plays Pokemon as a fun way to solve this gap, by using the game of Pokemon as an example of how to build a custom harness and tools for local models.
