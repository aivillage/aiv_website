DEF CON 34 runs August 6–9, 2026 in Las Vegas, NV. AI Village will be there with demos, CTF activity, and community programming for researchers, builders, defenders, sponsors, and volunteers working on practical AI security.

## Poster Session

AI Village's inaugural poster session at DEF CON 34 features research
on adversarial attacks against agents and agentic systems, including
threat models, evaluations, tooling, reproductions, negative results,
and work in progress.

Accepted posters are being added to the archive as final materials
arrive.
