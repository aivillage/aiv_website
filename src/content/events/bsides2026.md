BSidesSF 2026 took place March 21-22 in San Francisco. AI Village joined the community with hands-on AI security demos, workshop-style learning, and practitioner conversations around the use and abuse of artificial intelligence in security and privacy.

This was part of the RSA-week security community activity in San Francisco.

## What we did

- Interactive AI security demos and station-based learning
- Community conversations with researchers, builders, defenders, and volunteers
- Practical discussion around AI red teaming, model behavior, and security failure modes
