RSAC 2026 took place March 23-26 at Moscone Center in San Francisco. AI Village participated in the RSA-week security community with practical AI security education, demos, and conversations for attendees working at the intersection of AI, security, and privacy.

## What we did

- Hands-on AI security education
- Demos and discussion around AI vulnerabilities and red teaming
- Community conversations with practitioners, researchers, builders, and volunteers
